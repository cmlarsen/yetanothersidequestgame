# Genre Lessons — location-based / walking games

> What the successes prove and — more importantly — what the corpses teach.
> Companion to `DESIGN.md`. Written 2026-07; comps needn't be recent to be true.

## The headline autopsy: Harry Potter — Wizards Unite (2019 † 2022)

Died in under 3 years holding one of the biggest fantasy IPs on Earth, while
Pokémon GO still prints money. The five causes, each a design law for us:

1. **The core action had no tension.** PoGO's ball-flick has aim, timing,
   near-misses, "it broke out!" drama. WU's core verb was tracing a glyph and
   watching the same animation. *Law: the thing you do 100×/day must be a tiny
   game with skill expression and a gamble in it.*
2. **Collection without consequence.** WU collected stickers in an album;
   nothing collected changed how you played. In PoGO every catch is a potential
   battler / trade / candy. *Law: collection must feed power or it's homework.*
3. **It punished playing.** Spell Energy depleted on every action and refilled
   at inns or with money — an energy gate on the core verb. *Law: never charge
   the player for pressing the fun button. Soft, generous economies only.*
4. **Complexity wall at the door.** Potions, professions, skill trees,
   portkeys — before any fun. PoGO launched with one verb. *Law: progressive
   unlock; systems should arrive as the story introduces them.*
5. **It delivered the wrong fantasy.** The IP promised *being a wizard*; the
   game made you a magical janitor returning lost property. *Law: the fantasy
   on the tin must be the fantasy in the loop.* (Our veil conceit maps 1:1:
   monsters break through near you; you're the one who can see and act.)

## The meta-lesson (proven ≥4 times)

**IP is an acquisition tool, not a retention mechanic.** Wizards Unite,
Minecraft Earth († 2021), The Witcher: Monster Slayer († 2023), Niantic's own
NBA All-World and Marvel World of Heroes († 2023–24) all died with famous
brands. Loops retain; brands only invite. Liberating for us: no license needed.

## What's actually proven (steal shamelessly, these are mechanics not skins)

- **Collection with mechanical meaning** (PoGO) — checklist compulsion *plus*
  every acquisition matters. We have loot; a **bestiary** ("3 of 9 slime
  variants slain") is a cheap, on-fiction addition.
- **Distance-gated rewards** (PoGO eggs) — "hatches at 5 km" makes walking
  itself the timer. Natural layer on our EP system.
- **Background progress** (PoGO Adventure Sync — arguably its most important
  retention feature) — steps count while the app is closed. Our away-time
  systems are the first half; **HealthKit step-crediting is the killer feature
  of the native build.**
- **The real world as content** — biome / weather / time-of-day spawns (water
  monsters near rivers, wraiths at night). Going somewhere new = new content.
- **Audio-first play while moving** (Zombies, Run! — the quiet success) — the
  run itself is gameplay; no screen needed. Directly validates our voice-line /
  auto-battle direction.
- **Couch mode is not cheating** (Orna — the indie GPS-RPG with a devoted
  base) — walking is the *accelerator*, never the gate. We already honor this;
  keep it sacred. Corollary from PoGO's remote-raid saga: rigid location
  requirements are fragile, and *clawing back* convenience causes revolts.
- **Synchronized social events** (Community Days, raids) — the long-term
  retention backbone; matches our convergence/raids end-state.
- **Optional AR** — nobody uses PoGO's camera mode; mandatory phone-up AR
  helped kill Minecraft Earth. Never require it.

## SideQuest scorecard (2026-07)

| Law | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Core verb is a tiny game | ✅ accuracy, telegraphs, block/dodge — keep sharpening (see COMBAT-STUDY) |

| Progressive complexity | ⚠️ **our main WU risk** — camp now has class/forge/training/downtime/bastion/bounties/quests/inventory. Fine for kids who grew with it; a cold-start player hits a wall. Fix later via story-gated unlocks. |
| Deliver the promised fantasy | ✅ veil conceit = "defend your neighborhood" |
| Walking accelerates, never gates | ✅ sim mode is first-class |
| Background progress | ✅ away rewards + crafting; HealthKit next |
| Social layer | ⏳ deferred by design (raids / MUD vision) |

## Watch-list

- PoGO's real moat is **habit + social + live-ops cadence**, not novelty. A
  solo project can't out-cadence Niantic — but the veil's "convergence clock"
  is our native reason for evolving world-state without hand-built events.
- Complexity budget: every new camp system spends onboarding capital. Track it.
